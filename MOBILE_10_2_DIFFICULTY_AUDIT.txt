Summary
-------
• Registered games: 75

• Campaign games: 68 × 40 levels = 2,720 level constructions tested
• All games use the new per-account Game Pace setting: Relaxed, Comfortable, Standard, or Challenge.
• Comfortable is the default for new and existing accounts after migration.
• Crossy Street was specifically rebuilt for slower cars/logs and proper log carrying.

Crossy Street speed comparison
------------------------------
Previous level-1 lane speeds ranged roughly from 49 to 91 canvas pixels/second.
New level-1 road speeds range roughly from 31 to 47; logs range roughly from 28 to 30.
At level 40, road speeds cap around 45 to 61 and logs around 38 to 40 before the selected pace multiplier.
Comfortable pace applies a 0.86 multiplier, reducing those speeds further during normal play.

Important testing limitation
----------------------------
Automated tests verify startup, controls, engine construction, rendering, reaction time, and known target limits. They do not manually complete every possible level or every strategy path. The new Game Pace control lets a player immediately choose Relaxed if a particular game still feels too fast on their device.
