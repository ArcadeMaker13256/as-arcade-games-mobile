A'S ARCADE GAMES MOBILE 10.3 — SPEED AND DIFFICULTY AUDIT

USER-REPORTED ISSUE
The supplied Crossy Street video showed cars and logs moving too quickly for comfortable touchscreen reaction time.

GLOBAL SPEED FIX
All update-loop games now use these real-time multipliers:
- Very Slow: 0.20
- Relaxed: 0.30
- Comfortable: 0.40 (default)
- Standard: 0.55
- Challenge: 0.70

CROSSY STREET
- Initial safety shield: 4.0 seconds
- Post-move safety shield: 0.75 seconds
- Early levels: one moving object per lane
- Later levels: at most two moving objects per lane
- Wider spacing between cars/logs
- Wider logs and narrower cars
- Local lane speeds reduced drastically before the global pace multiplier is applied
