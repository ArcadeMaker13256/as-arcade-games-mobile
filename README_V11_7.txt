JOY-CON MOVEMENT FIX
- Prevents double-rotation of individual Joy-Con sticks in Chrome and other browsers that provide Standard Gamepad mapping.

- Supports a browser-reported combined Joy-Con device in Combined mode.
- Can split one browser-reported Joy-Con pair into Player 1 and Player 2 in Separate mode when four axes are available.
- Adds Automatic, rails-on-top, rails-on-bottom, and vertical holding-direction options.
- Keeps all Version 11.6 layout, countdown, drawing, touch, controller, autosave, and 100-game features.
